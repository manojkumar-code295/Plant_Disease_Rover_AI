🏎️ Motor Driver (L298N) GPIO Pinout

HARDWARE
⚠️ CRITICAL COMMON GROUND: Raspberry Pi Physical Pin 6 (GND) MUST connect directly to the L298N GND terminal. Without shared ground, control signals float and motors will not trigger!
Signal BCM GPIO Physical Pin Motor Function
IN1 GPIO 17 Pin 11 Left Motor Forward
IN2 GPIO 27 Pin 13 Left Motor Backward
IN3 GPIO 22 Pin 15 Right Motor Forward
IN4 GPIO 23 Pin 16 Right Motor Backward
ENA GPIO 12 Pin 32 Left Enable / PWM (or jumper ON)
ENB GPIO 13 Pin 33 Right Enable / PWM (or jumper ON)
PUMP GPIO 26 Pin 37 Spray Pump Relay
Manual Pin Toggle Command:
pinctrl set 17,27,22,23,12,13 op dh
