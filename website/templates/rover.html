Raspberry Pi 4 Model B

Verified BCM and Physical Board Pin mapping connecting the Raspberry Pi 4 to the L298N Dual H-Bridge Motor Driver, Pesticide Spray Pump Relay, Ultrasonic Distance Sensor, and CSI Camera.

Device BCM Pin # Function
PUMP_PIN GPIO 26 Pin 37 Relay Trigger (IN)

Wiring Tip: Ground (GND) is available on Physical Pins 6, 9, 14, 20, 25, 30, 34, 39. Ensure the L298N GND and Raspberry Pi GND share a common ground reference. Power the motors with an external 7.4V–12V battery pack, not directly from the Pi's 5V rail.
